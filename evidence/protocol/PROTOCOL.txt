18 tasks in TASKS.json are frozen before any new generation. Categories engineering/teaching/creative have six each. The frozen package and PACKAGE_FREEZE.json define EXPAND, REFINE, SAVE; no prompt changes after outputs. DIRECT_FULL uses the same baseline and continuation template with full P0/P1 candidate history. Each method has at most three independent generations, gpt-6.1-sol low effort, same output channel and capacity; identical actual request strings share response. Save all raw requests, attempts, failed usage/null, complete candidates and per-method generation tokens.
For each task, two joint anonymous reviews with reversed method order and stored label mapping. Each review assesses usable distinct primary mechanisms, serious hard-constraint violations, best written proposal and all six best-proposal pairs among four methods. Improvement is written-proposal judgment, not field effect. No post-hoc task/mode/threshold edits or extra arbitration. Summaries include all tasks, category means over six, wins/ties/losses, mechanism count differences, serious violations and input/output tokens/calls.
Core EXPAND threshold: at least two categories mean +1 mechanism vs DIRECT_FULL, third category mean difference >= -1; best quality mean win score >=0.5 with no new severe unusability tendency. REFINE and SAVE separately reported. Exact previously completed requests only are resumable; old development responses are never reused for these new tasks.
